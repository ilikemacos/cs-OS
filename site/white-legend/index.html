THE LEGEND

Five hundred winters after the fall雪 埋 聖 骨

When the Monkey King renounced his heavenly title, the Jade Court did not forgive. They shattered him into six relics and scattered them across the frozen west, sealing each beneath a yaoguai lord.

You are the Pale Wanderer, a white-furred ape born from the snow that fell on his grave. Take up the staff. Walk the old pilgrim road. Learn what the legends left out.

“The staff remembers every hand that held it. Be worthy of the weight.”— THE OLD ONE OF FLOWER-FRUIT PEAK
